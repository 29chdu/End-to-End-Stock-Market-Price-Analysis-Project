# End-to-End-Stock-Market-Price-Analysis-Project
The objective of this project is to analyze historical stock market data to identify price trends, trading patterns, volatility, and stock performance indicators. The analysis aims to provide insights that can assist investors and financial analysts in making informed investment decisions.

## BUSINESS PROBLEM
Stock prices fluctuate daily due to market conditions, economic events, and investor sentiment. Understanding historical stock behavior is essential for evaluating investment opportunities and managing financial risks.
#### This project seeks to answer the following questions:
  - Which stocks have the highest average closing prices?
  -	Which stocks exhibit the highest volatility?
  -	Are there noticeable trends in trading volume over time?
  -	What is the relationship between opening and closing prices?
  -	Which periods experienced significant price movements?

# Stock Price Analysis Report: 2014–2017

## 1. Introduction

This report provides a summary of stock market performance from 2014 to 2017 based on the Stock Price Analysis dashboard. The dashboard focuses on trading volume, closing prices, yearly stock performance, and the leading stock symbols by overall value.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators

During the analysis period:

* **Total Volume:** 2.12T
* **Average Closing Price:** 86.37
* **Highest Price:** 2.07K
* **Lowest Price:** 1.50
* **Percentage Change:** 3.04%

These indicators provide an overview of the overall stock market activity and price movements during the four-year period.

## 3. Stock Performance by Year

The dashboard shows a clear upward trend in total stock closing value from 2014 to 2017:

* **2014:** 9.42M
* **2015:** 10.41M
* **2016:** 10.68M
* **2017:** 12.45M

The strongest growth occurred between 2016 and 2017, when the value increased substantially to **12.45M**. This suggests that overall stock market performance improved significantly toward the end of the analysis period.

## 4. Top 10 Stock Symbols

The top-performing symbols shown on the dashboard are:

| Rank | Symbol |   Value |
| ---- | ------ | ------: |
| 1    | BAC    | 89.99bn |
| 2    | AAPL   | 45.49bn |
| 3    | GE     | 41.73bn |
| 4    | AMD    | 33.52bn |
| 5    | F      | 33.14bn |
| 6    | MSFT   | 30.93bn |
| 7    | FB     | 29.43bn |
| 8    | MU     | 28.16bn |
| 9    | CHK    | 28.08bn |
| 10   | INTC   | 27.35bn |

**BAC** recorded the highest value at **89.99bn**, significantly ahead of **AAPL**, which recorded **45.49bn**. This indicates that BAC had the strongest overall contribution among the top 10 symbols during the period.

## 5. Key Insights

The analysis highlights several important findings. First, the overall yearly stock value increased consistently from 2014 to 2017, indicating positive growth during the period. Second, 2017 recorded the highest yearly value at 12.45M. Third, BAC was the leading stock symbol, with a value of 89.99bn, while AAPL and GE ranked second and third respectively.

The dashboard also indicates that the market experienced considerable trading activity, with total volume reaching 2.12T.

## 6. Conclusion

Overall, the stock market demonstrated a positive performance trend between 2014 and 2017. The increase from **9.42M in 2014 to 12.45M in 2017** indicates significant growth over the analysis period. BAC was the leading symbol among the top 10 stocks, while several major companies, including AAPL, GE, AMD, MSFT, and FB, also recorded strong performance.

The dashboard can therefore be used as a high-level management tool for monitoring stock performance, identifying leading securities, and evaluating changes in market activity over time.

## Overall recommendation

The dashboard indicates positive overall stock-market growth from 2014 to 2017, with 2017 recording the strongest performance. However, before making investment decisions, the analysis should be extended beyond total values to include returns, volatility, trading volume, and individual stock trends. This would make the dashboard more useful for identifying both investment opportunities and potential risks.
